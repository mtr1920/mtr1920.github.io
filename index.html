OpsTrellis

Voice dictation for macOS and Linux. Hold a key, speak, let go, and clean text appears in any app. It transcribes offline by default, removes filler words, keeps code names like main.rs intact, and lets you bring your own cloud keys.

Rust, Tauri, TypeScript. Free and open source (MIT), v0.2.0.
